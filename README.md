This is a personal challenge I imposed on myself, recreating one of my very first games I did five years ago, back when I was studying, in just **five days**
The rules of my challenge are as follow:
- I only have five days to work on it, that is from February 6th of 2026 to February 10th.
- I am only allowed to use two keys/buttons besides the movement controls. Similar to many arcade machines and old console controller setups.
- I must conform with a small color palette for the whole game.
- Sprites can only have three colors max, while backgrounds can have four.
